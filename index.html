SynapShield

Exploratory reaction–diffusion model for a hypothetical localized α-synuclein sink

Research-only. This project is not a medical device, drug, diagnostic, prevention method, clinical protocol, or evidence that Parkinson’s disease can be stopped by a hydrogel. No person should act on this model medically.

What exists

A tested one-dimensional numerical model, a matched no-sink control, release-kinetic arithmetic, and uncertainty stress testing.

What does not exist

No measured binding constants, synthesized material, biological validation, safety data, animal evidence, clinical trial, or verified public preprint DOI.

Personal correction

Earlier versions falsely attributed Parkinson’s disease and quotations to private individuals. Those statements are retracted.

Current mathematical question

∂C/∂t = ∂/∂x(D(x) ∂C/∂x) - I_sink(x) Vmax C/(Km + C)

The candidate is compared with an identical control where Vmax = 0. A lower modeled concentration at an interface is not a clinical outcome.

Release audit

The former combined first-order rate was 1.6e-5 s^-1, implying a half-life near 12 hours—not 10–15 years.

Mechanics audit

A 10-second computation cannot validate 10–15 years or hundreds of millions of loading cycles. The current mechanics utility reports only a linear strain scale and explicitly prohibits fatigue extrapolation.

Evidence audit

The gut-first pathway is a research hypothesis applicable to some phenotypes, not a universal origin theorem. Observational associations for caffeine or NSAIDs are not implanted-dose efficacy evidence.
