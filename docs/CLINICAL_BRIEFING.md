# SynapShield clinical-status notice

## Retraction

The previous version of this file was not a valid clinical briefing. It contained unsupported or invented statements about:

- indications and eligibility criteria;
- an endoscopic procedure and dosing protocol;
- CPT and ICD coding;
- pharmacokinetic targets;
- efficacy endpoints;
- ISO biocompatibility results;
- adverse-event rates;
- trial phases, enrollment numbers, sites, and dates;
- reimbursement and coverage;
- FDA, IDE, PMA, IRB, and publication status;
- product samples, clinical availability, and patient use.

Those statements are retracted. They were not supported by experiments, regulatory records, verified trial registrations, or clinical data.

## Current evidence level

SynapShield currently consists of a synthetic one-dimensional reaction–diffusion hypothesis and supporting software tests. It has not established:

- that Parkinson's disease universally originates in the gut;
- that a local hydrogel can bind the relevant pathological α-synuclein species;
- that reducing a modeled local concentration changes propagation, neuronal survival, symptoms, progression, or disease risk;
- that the proposed material can be manufactured, retained, tolerated, dosed, or removed safely;
- any clinical indication, contraindication, procedure, endpoint, benefit, adverse-event rate, or treatment schedule.

The repository is not a source of medical advice or a protocol for human or animal intervention.

## Minimum evidence required before clinical language

### Molecular gate

Measure and report, for explicitly defined α-synuclein species:

- equilibrium affinity `Kd`;
- association and dissociation rates `kon` and `koff`;
- binding capacity `Bmax`;
- competitive binding in physiologic protein mixtures;
- retained or reduced seeding activity after capture;
- saturation and regeneration behavior.

### Material gate

Measure:

- composition and batch reproducibility;
- rheology and injection force;
- swelling, erosion, leachables, and degradation products;
- burst and sustained release under physiologic conditions;
- adhesion, migration, fatigue, and removal behavior;
- cytotoxicity and tissue compatibility under recognized standards.

### Transport gate

Calibrate the model to measured:

- diffusion coefficients;
- source fluxes or concentrations;
- tissue geometry;
- reaction rates;
- clearance terms;
- uncertainty distributions.

Demonstrate mesh and time convergence, conservation behavior, matched controls, parameter identifiability, and out-of-sample prediction.

### Biological gate

Demonstrate that the captured species and anatomical location are causally relevant in appropriate in-vitro, ex-vivo, and in-vivo systems. Include competing brain-first and body-first hypotheses rather than assuming one universal pathway.

### Regulatory and ethics gate

Any future animal or human work requires independent institutional review, appropriate regulatory authorization, qualified investigators, registered protocols where applicable, and verifiable documentation. None is asserted here.

## Permitted wording

Permitted:

> SynapShield is an exploratory computational hypothesis testing whether a localized saturable sink can reduce a synthetic concentration field under stated assumptions.

Not permitted without new evidence:

> SynapShield prevents, intercepts, slows, treats, or cures Parkinson's disease.

> SynapShield is safe, clinically validated, ready for trials, available, FDA approved, or supported by a verified IRB or preprint.
